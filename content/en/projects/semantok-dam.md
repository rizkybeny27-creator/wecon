**Semantok Dam** in Nganjuk Regency, East Java, is a National Strategic Project (PSN) officially inaugurated by the President of Indonesia in December 2022. Featuring a crest length of 3,100 meters, Semantok holds the record as the **longest earthfill dam in Southeast Asia**.

PT. WECON was entrusted by the Brantas River Basin Authority (BBWS Brantas) under the Ministry of PUPR as the primary *Construction Supervision Consultant* to ensure top-tier structural safety and engineering compliance.

---

### Technical Specifications Matrix

| Parameter | Specification Details |
|---|---|
| **Structure Type** | Zonal Clay-Core Earthfill Dam |
| **Crest Length** | 3,100 meters (Longest in Southeast Asia) |
| **Dam Height** | 38.5 meters from deep foundation |
| **Storage Capacity** | 32.67 million cubic meters (m³) |
| **Flood Reduction** | Reduces peak Nganjuk flood discharge by 30% |
| **Irrigation Supply** | Serves 1,900 hectares of local farmland |
| **Raw Water Supply** | 312 liters per second for municipal supply |
| **Client / Authority** | Ministry of PUPR — BBWS Brantas |

---

### Key Role & Regulatory Compliance

PT. WECON water engineers performed daily quality control, clay core compaction testing, and piezometer instrumentation monitoring.

The project strictly adhered to Indonesian [Dam Construction Permitting Services](https://weconsultant.id/en/services/dam-construction-permit) guidelines and [River Diversion Permitting Services](https://weconsultant.id/en/services/river-diversion-permit) for site dewatering operations.
