The **Jlantah Dam** located in Karanganyar Regency, Central Java, is a key strategic water infrastructure project supporting regional water and agricultural resilience.

Featuring a structure height of **70 meters** and an effective storage capacity of **10.97 million cubic meters (m³)**, the dam is designed for multi-purpose benefits including farmland irrigation, municipal raw water supply, and micro-hydro power potential.

---

### Technical Specifications Matrix

| Parameter | Specification Details |
|---|---|
| **Structure Type** | Zonal Clay-Core Earthfill Dam |
| **Dam Height** | 70 meters from foundation |
| **Crest Length** | 434 meters |
| **Storage Capacity** | 10.97 million cubic meters (m³) |
| **Irrigation Supply** | Serves 1,494 hectares of agricultural land |
| **Raw Water Supply** | 150 liters per second for municipal supply |
| **Client / Authority** | Ministry of PUPR — BBWS Bengawan Solo |
| **WECON Role** | DED Planning, Hydrological Modeling, & Construction Supervision |

---

### Key Role & Regulatory Compliance

PT. WECON led the Detailed Engineering Design (DED) phase, cofferdam hydraulic flow modeling, and site quality control supervision.

Technical compliance aligned with Indonesian [Dam Construction Permitting Services](https://weconsultant.id/en/services/dam-construction-permit) standards and [River Diversion Permitting Services](https://weconsultant.id/en/services/river-diversion-permit) during initial river closure.
