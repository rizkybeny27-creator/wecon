A **Surface Water Intake Permit (SIPPA)** is a mandatory legal authorization for commercial, industrial, or hydropower projects extracting river water in Indonesia. Applications are processed through the official Ministry of PUPR portal **SIPPA / SIP SDA**.

---

## What is SIPPA (SIP SDA)?

**SIPPA** is the official online licensing portal managed by the Directorate General of Water Resources, Ministry of PUPR ([perizinansda.pu.go.id](https://perizinansda.pu.go.id/)). It handles:
- Hydropower (PLTA / PLTM) Water Intake Permits
- Industrial Water Supply & Factory Intake
- Commercial Water Supply & PDAM
- Irrigation & Agricultural Extraction

---

## Technical Requirements for SIPPA Licensing

To receive technical recommendations from Regional River Basin Authorities (BBWS/BWS), applicants must provide:

1. **Water Balance Study:** Flow Duration Curve (FDC) calculations matching dependable river flow vs intake demand.
2. **Intake Structure DED:** Detailed Engineering Design for flood-resistant intake gates, pumps, and sediment traps.
3. **AMDAL / UKL-UPL Clearance:** Environmental impact studies on downstream flow preservation.
