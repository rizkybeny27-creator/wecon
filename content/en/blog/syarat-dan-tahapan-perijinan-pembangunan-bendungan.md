Dam construction in Indonesia involves high-hazard infrastructure regulated strictly by the Ministry of PUPR under Ministerial Regulation No. 27/PRT/M/2015.

---

## 4 Stages of Dam Permitting in Indonesia

Dam construction licenses are granted in four distinct phases approved by the Minister of PUPR based on Dam Safety Commission (KKB) evaluations:

### 1. Dam Design Safety Certification
Before construction begins, the engineering design must undergo evaluation by the Dam Safety Commission. Requirements include:
- Dam Break Analysis & Inundation Mapping
- Seismic Hazard & Geotechnical Foundation Studies
- Physical Hydraulic Model Testing for Spillways

### 2. Construction Execution Authorization
Following design certification, proponents apply for a Construction Permit supported by Quality Management Plans, Cofferdam DED, and Dam Safety Plans.

### 3. Initial Reservoir Impounding Permit
Prior to filling the reservoir, KKB experts perform field inspections of piezometer instrumentation, seepage behavior, and Emergency Action Plans (EAP).

### 4. Long-Term Dam Operation Permit
Granted after successful initial impounding evaluation and confirmed structural stability.
