A **River Diversion Permit** (or *izin pengalihan alur sungai*) is an official authorization required from the Indonesian Ministry of Public Works and Public Housing (PUPR) via the **Regional River Basin Authority (BBWS/BWS)** before diverting natural river channels for dam construction, mining, bridges, or hydropower projects.

Under **PUPR Ministerial Regulation No. 4/2024 on River Channel Diversion** (replacing PUPR Regulation No. 21/2020), proponents must obtain official technical recommendations proving the new diversion channel provides equal or superior flood conveyance safety.

---

## 1. Key Technical Requirements

Before applying for BWS technical recommendations, project developers must prepare:

- **Feasibility & Location Maps:** Maps of the existing river bed, temporary diversion channel, and permanent new channel.
- **Hydrology & Hydraulics Studies:** Design flood calculations for 25 to 100-year return periods and water surface profile modeling using HEC-RAS software.
- **Diversion Structure Design:** Detail Engineering Design (DED) for cofferdams, diversion tunnels, or open channels with slope stability analysis.
- **Environmental Impact Assessment (AMDAL/UKL-UPL):** Water quality and Total Suspended Solids (TSS) mitigation strategies during river closure.

---

## 2. Technical Recommendation Procedure at BWS/BBWS

1. **Topographic & Bathymetric Field Surveys:** Measuring cross-sections of original and proposed river channels.
2. **Technical Report Preparation:** Formulated and certified by licensed water resource engineers.
3. **BBWS/BWS Panel Presentation:** Technical presentation before Ministry experts.
4. **Issuance of Technical Recommendation:** Formal authorization enabling river closure.
